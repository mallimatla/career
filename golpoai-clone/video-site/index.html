Frequently Asked Questions

What is Golpo Video?

+

Golpo Video is an AI-powered platform that transforms documents, PDFs, and text prompts into engaging whiteboard animation videos. Our advanced AI automatically generates scripts, animations, and voiceovers in 50+ languages.

How does it work?

+

Simply upload your document or paste your text, and our AI will analyze the content, create a script, generate animations, and add a voiceover. You can then customize colors, edit the script, and fine-tune every aspect of your video.

What file formats are supported?

+

We support PDF, DOCX, TXT, and direct text input. You can also provide URLs to web content that you'd like to convert into videos.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

+

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time. Your access will continue until the end of your current billing period.

Do you offer refunds?

+

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for all paid plans. If you're not satisfied, contact our support team for a full refund.

What happens if I exceed my video limit?

+

You can upgrade to a higher plan or purchase additional videos as needed. We'll notify you when you're approaching your limit.

Can I customize the voiceover?

+

Yes! You can choose from multiple voice options, adjust speed, pitch, and even upload your own voiceover on Professional plans and above.

Can I add my company logo?

+

Yes, Professional and higher plans allow you to add custom logos and brand colors to your videos.

How many languages are supported?

+

We support 50+ languages with natural-sounding voiceovers, including English, Spanish, French, German, Chinese, Japanese, and many more.

Is there an API available?

+

Yes, API access is available on Business and Agency plans. Contact our sales team for documentation and integration support.

What video formats can I export?

+

Videos can be exported in MP4, MOV, and WebM formats at various resolutions including 720p, 1080p, and 4K.

How long does it take to generate a video?

+

Most videos are generated within 2-5 minutes, depending on length and complexity. You'll receive a notification when your video is ready.
